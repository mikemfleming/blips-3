Blips

A living instrument. Conway’s Game of Life unfolds on 512 × 512 cells. Invisible 32 × 32 music regions turn their density into sound.

Play scans left to right in sixteenth notes. Stabby articulates each step; Smooth joins neighboring active steps in the same pitch row. Stereo follows the scanner at a fixed 66% width. Edges wrap around. Tortoise is 30 BPM; Hare is 90 BPM.

Select the pencil to draw or erase with a 3-cell-wide stroke, even while playing. Drawn cells stay alive for 16 Conway generations, then resume normal rules. Erasing and clearing remove them immediately. Random gives every cell an independent 50% chance of life. The trash button clears the grid. Space toggles playback.

The playhead moves every sixteenth note. Conway evolves independently at roughly the same interval; the canvas shows each completed generation on the next screen refresh. The rainbow redraws at display refresh while playing. Generations do not wait for the end of a sweep.
