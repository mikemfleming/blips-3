Blips of Life

Let's turn an evolving pattern into music. Draw something and roll the dice then listen as the pattern evolves.

The pattern is built up from organisms and cells. These are components of Conway's Game of Life and you'll see that they interact with each other in surprising and fun ways. The result is actually not random, whether a cell lives or dies is determined by how many living neighbors it has. Cells form organisms that can move across the grid, explode with life, pulse, or stay in place.

The rainbow scanner reads one column at a time, from left to right. Living cells in each region sound a note like a cat on a keyboard. Higher rows make higher notes, lower rows make lower notes, and more living cells make the note louder.
