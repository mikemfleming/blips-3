Blips

A living instrument. Conway’s Game of Life unfolds on 512 × 512 cells. Invisible 32 × 32 music regions turn their density into sound.

Play scans left to right in sixteenth notes. Stabby articulates each step; Smooth joins neighboring active steps in the same pitch row. Edges wrap around.

Select the pencil to pause and draw or erase cells. Random gives every cell an independent 50% chance of life. The trash button clears the grid. Space toggles playback.
